FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Everything we hear most often. Can't find it here? Get in touch.

What size is the print?

Each panel is A4, 210 × 297mm, currently the only size we offer. Want something bigger right now? See the next question, you can combine multiple A4 panels into a larger piece. We're also planning to introduce A3 panels in the future for those who want a bigger single panel.

Can I combine multiple prints into a bigger display?

Yes, that's the idea. On the Create page, choose Single (1 panel), Duo (2 panels), or Quad (4 panels). Upload one image and we automatically split it across every panel you've picked. Each panel ships and mounts separately, on its own magnetic pad, but placed edge to edge on your wall they read as one large piece.

What images can I upload?

Anything that means something to you: a car, a pet, a wedding, a wild place, artwork you made or own the rights to use. See "Can I use artwork I found online?" below if you're not sure.

What image quality do I need?

Aim for at least 2100 × 3000 pixels for a single A4 panel, more if you're combining panels into a Duo or Quad. The sharper your original file, the sharper your VANTA. The Create page checks your upload automatically and tells you before you pay if it's not going to print well.

What happens if my image isn't good enough?

We'll tell you before you pay, not after. You'll see a clear warning with the option to upload a better version, or continue anyway at your own discretion.

Can you crop my image?

Yes. The Create page lets you position and zoom your image inside the print frame, so you see exactly what will print before you confirm your order.

How long does production take?

3 to 5 business days from confirmed payment to dispatch. (Placeholder figure.)

How does delivery work?

Nationwide courier, tracked door to door, 2 to 4 business days after dispatch. (Placeholder figure.)

How do I mount the print?

Your VANTA is its own mount. A slim adhesive-backed magnetic pad sticks to the wall, no drilling, no screws, and the metal print simply attaches to it, held by its own material. Reposition or remove it anytime without leaving a mark.

Can I order more than one?

Yes, simply repeat the Create process for each image. There's no bundle checkout yet, so each goes through as its own order.

What file formats are accepted?

JPG and PNG, whichever is the highest resolution version you have.

Can I use artwork I found online?

Only if you own it or have the right to reproduce it. VANTA prints your image, we don't hold rights to anyone else's work, so that part is on you. We'll ask you to confirm this before your order goes through.
