How it works

From your image to your wall, simply.

No catalogue to browse, no design decisions to make. You send the image, we handle the rest. Here is exactly what happens.

The process

Three steps, start to finish.

01

Choose your image

Upload the photograph, artwork, or design you want transformed, on the Create page. JPG or PNG, the highest resolution version you have. We check it automatically and tell you before you pay if it won't print sharp.

02

We create your VANTA

Your image is permanently sublimated into a premium coated steel panel, vivid colour, sharp detail, a durable satin finish that resists fading and scratching.

03

Display it

Your finished piece ships ready to display: a slim adhesive magnetic pad goes on the wall, no drilling, and the print simply attaches to it, held by its own metal. No frame, no glass, no glare.

The product

What is a VANTA print, exactly?

Dimensions

A4, 210 × 297mm per panel, currently our only available size. Want something bigger? Order a Duo (2 panels) or Quad (4 panels) on the Create page, we split your image cleanly across them, mounted edge to edge as one large piece. A3 panels are planned for a future release.

Material & finish

Specially coated steel panel, dye-sublimated so the image becomes part of the surface rather than sitting on top of it. Satin finish, 1.5mm thick, and magnetic, which is how it mounts.

The process

Your image is printed onto transfer paper with sublimation ink, then heat-pressed onto the panel. Heat turns the ink to gas, which bonds into the coating, permanent, not just printed on.

Going bigger

Combine panels into one large piece.

A single A4 is the base unit, but on the Create page you can choose a Duo (2 panels) or Quad (4 panels). Upload one image, and we automatically split it across every panel you've chosen, positioned so the seams line up.

Each panel still ships and mounts individually, on its own magnetic pad, so hanging a Quad is just four quick, simple placements rather than one heavy, awkward piece.

Before you upload

Getting the sharpest result.

Metal rewards a good source image and shows up a weak one. A few things to know before you upload.

Resolution

Aim for at least 2100 × 3000px for a single A4 panel, more if you're combining panels into a Duo or Quad. The Create page checks your file automatically and warns you if it's under that, before you pay.

Source over screenshot

Use the original photo file where you can. A screenshot of a photo, or a photo of a screen, loses sharpness twice over.

Light and focus

A well-lit, in-focus image will always outprint a dim or blurry one, no amount of processing on our end can add back detail that was never captured.

Mounting

No frame, no drilling, no screws. A slim adhesive-backed magnetic pad sticks to your wall, and the print itself clicks onto it, held by its own metal. Lift it off and move it anytime, no marks left behind.

Care

Wipe with a soft, dry or barely damp cloth. No abrasive cleaners. Keep out of direct, prolonged sun to preserve colour over decades.
