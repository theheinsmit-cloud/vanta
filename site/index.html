More on how it works

Think bigger

One image. Multiple panels. One big piece.

Every VANTA is a single A4 panel, but you're not limited to one. Order 2 or 4 and we split your image cleanly across them. Mounted edge to edge with only a hairline gap, they read as one large artwork, not four separate prints.

  • Choose Single, Duo, or Quad right on the Create page, no extra steps.
  • One image, automatically positioned across every panel you choose.
  • Each panel still mounts on its own magnetic pad, simple to hang, simple to adjust.
